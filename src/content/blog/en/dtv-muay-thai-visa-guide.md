In brief:
The DTV Muay Thai visa lets Muay Thai enthusiasts stay legally in Thailand for up to 180 days per entry, with one renewal possible. This guide covers the eligibility criteria, required documents, step-by-step application process, and common pitfalls to avoid.

*Information verified as of November 2026. Rules may change: always check with the relevant embassy for the latest updates.*

## What is the DTV Muay Thai Visa and Who Is It For?

The DTV (Destination Thailand Visa) is a five-year multiple-entry visa designed for various profiles, including Muay Thai practitioners. It's ideal for those wishing to train at a registered Muay Thai school or camp in Thailand for longer than a standard tourist visa allows, without working locally.

**Who can apply for the DTV Muay Thai visa?**
- Muay Thai practitioners (from beginner to advanced)
- Trainees at registered camps or schools
- Participants in long-term programmes (several weeks to months)
- Foreign adults (minors can be included as dependants)

**Note:** The DTV Muay Thai visa does not permit you to work for a Thai company or club.

## What Are the Requirements for the DTV Muay Thai Visa?

To be eligible for the DTV Muay Thai visa, you must meet several strict criteria:

- **Enrolment in a registered Muay Thai camp or school** (detailed programme, official admission letter required)
- **Stable bank balance:** Minimum 500,000 THB (approx. €15,000) maintained over the last 3 months
- **Proof of permanent residence** in the country where you are applying
- **Recent criminal record check**
- **Complete and coherent application** (see checklist below)

> **Note:** Language schools or unregistered camps are not accepted.

## What Documents Do You Need for a Strong Application?

Here’s the checklist of documents required for the DTV Muay Thai visa:

| Document | Details |

| Passport | Valid for at least 6 months (12 months recommended) |

## What Are the Steps to Apply for the DTV Muay Thai Visa?

1. **Check your eligibility** (registered camp, sufficient funds, residence, criminal record)
2. **Prepare your documents** (see checklist above)
3. **Get your application checked** ([document check for €99](/en/visa-dtv/verification-dossier/), highly recommended to avoid mistakes)
4. **Submit your application online** at thaievisa.go.th, from outside Thailand
5. **Choose the correct embassy** (country of nationality or permanent residence)
6. **Follow up and provide additional documents if requested**
7. **Receive your visa and plan your trip**

**Processing times:**
- Preparing your file: a few days after receiving all documents
- Embassy processing: varies, from 1 to several weeks (Paris is often slower)

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid with the DTV Muay Thai Visa

Most refusals are due to:
- Recently deposited funds (rejected for "fund parking")
- Incomplete or weak documentation (vague admission letter, unregistered camp)
- Missing proof of residence or criminal record
- Applying from within Thailand or at the wrong embassy

**Insider tips:**
- Choose a reputable camp that provides a detailed letter (programme, duration, contact details, price)
- Prepare your documents in advance, especially the criminal record check
- Do not artificially inflate your bank account: embassies check for stable balances over 3 months

## Can You Bring Your Family?

Yes, the DTV Muay Thai visa allows your legal spouse and children under 20 to apply as dependants. Each must submit their own application with proof of family relationship.

## How to Maximise Your Chances?

- Complete application, strong supporting documents (clear admission letter)
- Stable funds, no recent large deposits
- Pre-check ([document check for €99](/en/visa-dtv/verification-dossier/))
- Strictly follow the chosen embassy’s instructions (some rules may vary)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply from within Thailand?
No, you must apply from outside Thailand, at the embassy in your country of nationality or permanent residence.

### Do I need a minimum level in Muay Thai?
No, but you must be enrolled in an official programme recognised by Thai authorities.

### How long can I stay with the DTV Muay Thai visa?
Up to 180 days per entry, renewable once (up to 360 consecutive days), within a five-year period.

### Can I work in Thailand with this visa?
No, you cannot work for a Thai company, camp, or Thai clients.

### What if my application is refused?
There are no refunds, but you can review the reason for refusal and submit a stronger application next time.
